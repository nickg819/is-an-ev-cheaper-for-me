Is an EV cheaper for me?

The basics
Look up your state's average on AAA gas prices (opens in a new tab).
Charging
Your utility bill has your exact rate. For a state average, see EIA residential prices (opens in a new tab). They are listed in cents, so 16 cents is 0.16.
See average public charging prices by state on AAA EV charging prices (opens in a new tab). The app of the network you use shows its own rate.
Optional

Vehicle data from the EPA and DOE at fueleconomy.gov. Maintenance figures are estimates, with their sources listed under each car. The only thing sent anywhere is the year, make and model you pick, which goes to the EPA lookup.
