Is an EV cheaper for me?

Basics

Gas price: look up your state's average on AAA gas prices (opens in a new tab).

Charging

Home electricity: your utility bill has your exact rate. For a state average, see EIA residential prices (opens in a new tab). They are listed in cents, so 16 cents is 0.16.

Fast charging: see average public charging prices by state on AAA EV charging prices (opens in a new tab). The app of the network you use shows its own rate.

Optional

Vehicle data from the EPA and DOE at fueleconomy.gov. Maintenance figures are estimates, with their sources listed under each car. The EPA lookup gets the year, make and model you pick, and the page loads its font from Google. Nothing else leaves your browser.
